## Part 1 — Code: ranked fix list

### Critical

**C1. Track logging never gets its foreground service / wake lock.**
`lib/services/track_recorder.dart:50-62`, `lib/map/map_screen.dart:108-111`
`geolocator_android` caches one platform stream; the *first* subscriber's settings win. `MapScreen` (in an `IndexedStack`, never unmounted) subscribes in `initState` with plain `LocationSettings`, so when "Log track" starts, its `AndroidSettings(foregroundNotificationConfig, enableWakeLock, distanceFilter, interval)` are silently discarded. Pocketed phone → Android throttles the process → gappy or empty tracks. (The 2.2 km emulator test passed because the emulator screen never slept.)
Fix: one app-level `LocationHub` owns the platform stream; `start()` tears down all subscriptions, re-subscribes with the foreground config, and the map/capture screens re-attach through the hub.

### High

**H1. Restore can re-apply on every launch and wipe records made since.**
`lib/backup/restore.dart:106-121,149-150`, `lib/main.dart:34-45`
`READY` marker is only removed at the end of the unawaited, un-try/caught `remapRestoredMedia`. If the app is killed or one blob copy throws mid-remap, next launch sees `READY` and overwrites the live DB (including anything recorded since) with the staged snapshot again — forever, if the throw is deterministic. Fix: consume `READY` immediately after the DB swap; make remap idempotent and error-tolerant.

**H2. SQLite sidecar files not swapped on restore.** `restore.dart:115-119`
A hot `-journal` from a killed session gets "rolled back" into the freshly restored DB → corruption. Move `-journal/-wal/-shm` with the main file; run `PRAGMA integrity_check` before writing `READY`.

**H3. Unlocated captures are written at the property centroid, unflagged.** `capture_screen.dart:144-146,169`
`gpsAccuracyM = -1` is only written when the property has *no* centroid. With a boundary imported and no fix, records get the centroid with `gpsAccuracyM = null` and display as real coordinates. Contradicts the on-screen "none — flagged, not faked" and the export contract. Fix: always write `-1` when `fix == null`.

**H4. Undo tombstones the observation only.** `app_shell.dart:61-71`
Photo files, `media`, `media_links`, and `env_context` rows survive → counted by backup, included in exports. Also fire-and-forget with no error handling.

**H5. Capture `_save` has no error handling; observation inserted before the photo is written.** `capture_screen.dart:135-219`
A photo write failure leaves a photo-less record and the screen stuck on "WRITING…"; user re-shoots → duplicate. Wrap in try/finally; write photo first or wrap both in one transaction.

**H6. Camera controller leaks if capture is cancelled during `initialize()`.** `capture_screen.dart:84-86`, `ghost_capture_screen.dart:76-78`
`if (!mounted) return;` without disposing → "camera in use" until process restart.

**H7. MBTiles precedence is file-existence, not tile-count; no removal path.** `map_screen.dart:66-81`, `area_downloader.dart:106`
A capture that fails on tile 1 leaves an empty `basemap.mbtiles` → blank map, and Offline Maps can't fix it. A successful capture also hides the whole sideloaded county PMTiles. Fix: merged endpoint (MBTiles → PMTiles fallback); prefer MBTiles only when `tileCount > 0`; add "remove captured areas".

**H8. `MapScreen` async init has no `mounted` guards.** `map_screen.dart:59-113,138-144`
Property switch / post-capture rebuild during `await` leaks the tile server (bound to a closed DB → 500s) and the GPS subscription (which, per C1, pins the plain-settings stream forever).

**H9. `observed_at` displayed as UTC with the local zone label.** `record_detail_screen.dart:162`
Stored UTC (correct), printed raw with `localTzName()` appended → "18:48 CDT" at 13:48 CDT. Observed live on the phone. Convert with `DateTime.parse(...).toLocal()` before formatting.

### Medium

- **M1** `pmtiles_reader.dart:38-47` — `HttpRangeSource` accepts HTTP 200 and buffers the whole body (planet file → OOM); no timeout; no cancel on `AreaDownloader`.
- **M2** `env_context.dart:112-140` — Open-Meteo archive lags days; trailing nulls coerced to `0.0`, `is_stale` cleared → "0 mm / N days since rain" committed as final on the common path. Leave stale when the last day is null.
- **M3** `area_downloader.dart:109-123`, `mbtiles_store.dart:50-56` — one HTTP round-trip + one autocommit insert per tile on the UI isolate; batch in transactions, coalesce Hilbert-adjacent ranges, add cancel.
- **M4** `mbtiles_store.dart:17` — reader/writer share the file with no `busy_timeout` → `SQLITE_BUSY` tile holes while panning during capture. `PRAGMA busy_timeout=2000; journal_mode=WAL`.
- **M5** `keyring.dart:105`, `restore.dart:80-87` — recovery phrase not whitespace-normalised or `validateMnemonic`'d; a phrase pasted with a line break fails with "wrong passphrase".
- **M6** `backup_engine.dart:35,55-56` — `generationsToKeep` is dead code; DB generations accumulate forever on-device and in the shared zip.
- **M7** `exporter.dart:106-127`, `map_screen.dart:281-296` — GeoJSON and the map plot `-1`-flagged records at their fake lat/lng. Emit `geometry: null`.
- **M8** `exporter.dart:31,40-45` — per-property export's `database.sqlite` contains every property and all tombstones (privacy leak under D-003 multi-property).
- **M9** `backup_screen.dart:82,92-103` — no passphrase confirmation on keyring creation; toggling encryption off/on silently mints a new keyring, orphaning prior encrypted generations.
- **M10** `ledger_tab.dart:53-65`, `species_tab.dart:69-114`, grow bodies — streams recreated in `build` → "no entries" flash on every rebuild; per-row futures re-fetch thumbnails.
- **M11** `ledger_tab.dart:42-49`, `species_tab.dart:39-65` — tabs don't react to property switch; species occurrence counts never refresh until restart.
- **M12** plantings/planting_detail/features/photo_points/programs/main — validation runs *after* the sheet pops; invalid forms silently discard everything the user typed.
- **M13** `planting_detail_screen.dart:110-129`, `survival.dart:36-43` — alive > planted accepted → "150%"; survival denominator is tagged plants, not `count_planted` (spec §4.8). Decide and label.

- **M15** `kml_import_screen.dart:70-153` — commit is non-transactional, unhandled errors leave `_committing` stuck; re-run duplicates zones. `MultiGeometry` of points keeps only the first; `lon, lat` with a space is silently dropped.

- **M19** `photo_points_screen.dart:136-142`, `features_screen.dart:110-119` — up to 10 s silent GPS wait after the dialog, FAB still live → duplicates; no permission request.
- **M20** `app_shell.dart:54-71` — save snackbar coded for 3.6 s but observed persisting >3 min across every tab, covering the Grow tab's add-FABs. Root cause not yet isolated (suspect the messenger re-animating across Scaffolds resets the timer); reproduce and fix.

### Low (grouped)

- Loopback tile server unauthenticated; serves whole basemap dir incl. `basemap.mbtiles` whose metadata bounds = property bbox (`tile_server.dart:30,57-61`). Add a per-launch token.
- `INTERNET` not declared in app manifest (works via plugin merge today).
- Track recorder: no permission check, dead `lastError`, no crash recovery for open tracks, no accuracy gate on points.
- Map GPS stream runs at best-accuracy, unfiltered, on every tab for the app's lifetime.
- `tileCompression` parsed but not honoured; unguarded `onStyleLoadedCallback` (one bad zone row kills the position dot).
- Restore trusts manifest paths/hashes as filenames in `plain` scheme; no AAD binding of blobs; no plaintext-hash check on restore; no `schema_version` in manifest; Argon2id (64 MiB×3) runs pure-Dart on the UI isolate; missing blobs listed in inventory.
- Restored media get no thumbnails and assume `.jpg`.

- Duplicate tag codes accepted; `countCurrent` can go negative; logging an activity on a complete practice regresses it; `unit` stored as `''`.
- Raw SQL interpolation of property id in `species_tab.dart:50-54` (UUID today; use `Variable`).
- Grow bodies' extended FABs stack ~4 px under the shell CaptureFab; lists lack bottom padding.
- "Take my data" has no busy guard. Sheet/dialog `TextEditingController`s never disposed. Exports accumulate under `<docs>/exports/`. `PassphraseCipher` produced only by tests and rejected by restore — wire or delete.

## Part 2 — UX walkthrough (first-time user, Pixel 9 Pro, in the yard)

Method: cold open on the Map tab, task = "log the live oak next to me". Did not test track logging or the ghost overlay (can't walk). Test record left in the ledger: *Quercus fusiformis*, note "UX test - delete me".

### Blocking / confusing

1. **Can't find yourself.** Map opens on the property; the "GPS" badge is a decorative blinking diamond, not a button; there is no locate-me control. If you're not standing where the map is looking, you're lost. → Real locate button, dot-on-open, and make the badge read as status ("GPS ±5 m"), not a control.
2. **Can't add a second place.** "ADD A PLACE" exists only on the zero-properties screen. The switcher sheet lists places with no add action. D-003's multi-property UI is unreachable after first run.
3. **Can't fix a record.** Detail offers EDIT NOTES and DELETE only. Wrong species or type → delete and re-shoot. In the field, mis-IDs are the norm.
4. **Wrong time on the record.** "18:48 CDT" at 13:48 (H9). A user will distrust every timestamp after seeing that once.
5. **Save toast never goes away** and hides the Grow add buttons (M20).

### Jargon leaking into the UI (all observed on screen)

- Capture header: `ZONE · POINT-IN-POLYGON · TURF_DART` — that's the geometry library's package name. Should be the zone name, or "outside any zone".
- Callout: "A machine ID is never written to taxon_id without your acceptance…" — schema-speak, and it describes M5 (not built). Remove until species ID exists.
- `OBSERVATION_TYPE`, `OBSERVED_AT`, `LAT / LNG`, `ZONE_ID`, `PRECIP_30D`, `upload_state = local`, `UPLOAD_STATE = LOCAL · NOTHING HAS LEFT THE PHONE`, `— END OF LOCAL STORE —`, `56 FAVOURITED PALETTE · 123 REGIONAL SEED · TYPO-TOLERANT`, `Incremental, content-addressed`, `PMTILES ARCHIVE · SERVED FROM 127.0.0.1 · RANGE REQUESTS`, `LLM RE-RANK KEY · A CLAUDE PRO PLAN IS NOT API ACCESS`, `PL@NTNET · LICENSING PENDING`.
- The press aesthetic can carry monospace labels, but they should be *words* ("Seen", "Where", "Zone", "Rain, 30 d"). Keep the privacy sentiment ("nothing has left the phone") — drop the `=`.
- "1 ENTRIES", "0 MIN" → "1 entry", "just now".

### Friction in the capture flow

- Camera step: "READY" (for what?), "±5.4 M" unlabelled, raw lat/lng in the corner, a centre reticle with no purpose, no photo/no-flash/flip controls. Fine for a power user; a first-timer wants "GPS good · tap to shoot".
- No thumbnail of the photo just taken on the form step — you can't tell if you got the leaf or your boot.
- Species field is a blank text box: no favourites list, no browse, no hint that common names work. Autocomplete *does* work (typed "live" → Plateau Live Oak ★), but nothing tells you it will. Show favourites as chips before typing.
- After picking a species the keyboard stays up over the type list, the "IDENTIFICATION · NOTHING WRITTEN YET" kicker doesn't update, and the type stays GENERAL — picking a plant should default the type to PLANT.
- The 10 observation-type pills are full-width and stack vertically → scroll to see them all. A 2-column chip wrap fits on one screen.
- "CONFIDENCE CERTAIN" appears on the record though I never said so. Either ask (Certain / Probable / Unsure) or don't claim it.
- Notes step is titled "NOTES & VOICE" with no voice control; field not auto-focused (extra tap); the multiline field's enter key is a newline so there's no keyboard-level "done".
- After save the app jumps to the Ledger. Walking a yard tagging plants, you want to stay on the map and shoot again. Stay put; show the toast; let the Ledger badge count.
- Android back on the form step discards the photo and form silently (M18).

### Ledger / detail / species

- Ledger row leads with the Latin name though I typed the common name; coordinates instead of a place ("Front yard", "outside any zone"). Common name first for this demographic; Latin as the secondary line.
- Detail: photo header has no scrim so the status bar is unreadable on a dark photo; "SOIL Urban land ·" trails a dot with nothing after it (empty field rendered).
- Species tab: every row wears a NATIVE pill (noise when all are native — show only the exceptions); no visual difference between favourited and seed taxa; no occurrence counts. Alphabetical by Latin, so a user looking for "bluestem" scans Andropogon.
- Settings: "BACKUP CURRENT — UNVERIFIED · 8 D AGO" right after adding a record — it isn't current. The card is otherwise the best-written thing in the app ("an untested backup is not a backup").

### What works

- Capture-to-saved is fast (0.7 s) and the FAB is unmissable. Autocomplete is good. Env context (rain, days since rain, soil series) landing on the record automatically is the kind of thing that sells the app. The paper/ink design is distinctive and readable outdoors-bright. Empty states have voice. The privacy line on save is the right instinct.
